Scale and spacing

Post titles: 36-52px. Article titles: 56-112px. Body: 16-20px with 1.6-1.8 line height. Metadata: 11-12px. Display tracking: about -0.025em to -0.035em.

Use italic with purpose

Use Instrument Serif italic for brief emphasis within a serif headline. Leave at least 1.1 line height and space below descenders. Keep long reading passages in Geist.

How to use the brand.

  • Give the content space. The homepage is a simple writing list with dates, titles, and summaries. Articles are text only, with no cover artwork.
  • Make lime useful. Use it for primary actions, selection, and the favicon. Use ink for link text on paper; lime is too light for small text on paper.
  • Keep one shape system. 12px for small assets, 16px for imagery and specimen panels, and 999px for pill controls. Use thin neutral dividers.
  • Keep the voice direct. Louder favours short, informal, often lowercase language. The portfolio keeps its original titles and proper-name capitalisation.
  • Use motion as feedback. A small arrow shift on writing links is enough. Use Louder’s cubic-bezier(.22,.7,.24,1) easing and honour reduced motion.

Where this came from.

Reference: trylouder.ai, inspected on 16 September 2026. Colours were extracted from the live --louder-* variables and checked against rendered page styles. Font families, serif emphasis, pill actions, and thin borders were also verified in the browser.

Source stylesheet: 0.GcIKl7oQ.css. This hashed asset may change on future Louder deployments; this document records the values at the time of the audit.

Original portfolio audit: paper #F4F2E9, navy #101D3B, rust #B54A32, Avenir/system typography, square imagery, and heavy dividers. The update keeps the homepage, /writing/building-this-blog, copy, and link destinations. The homepage uses a writing list; articles are text only.

Design settings: DESIGN_VARIANCE: 5, MOTION_INTENSITY: 3, VISUAL_DENSITY: 3. A restrained refresh of the existing editorial layout, implemented in the current React + native CSS stack.

Fonts are self-hosted Latin WOFF2 files from Google Fonts with SIL Open Font License notices alongside them. Geist and Geist Mono are variable fonts; Instrument Serif includes regular and italic. Fallback fonts handle glyphs outside these subsets.
